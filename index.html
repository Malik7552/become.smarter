🔥 Study Tips

⏰

Use a Timer

Study for a focused period, then take a short break. Try 25 minutes of studying and 5 minutes of rest.

📱

Remove Distractions

Put your phone away or turn on Do Not Disturb while studying.

🧠

Active Recall

Close your book and try to explain what you learned without looking at your notes.

✍️

Take Notes

Write down important ideas using short points instead of copying everything.

🔄

Review Often

Review your lessons regularly instead of trying to memorize everything the night before.

💧

Take Care of Yourself

Drink water, take breaks, and get enough sleep so your brain can work properly.

📅 Simple Study Routine

  • ✅ 1. Choose one subject.
  • ✅ 2. Set a timer for 25 minutes.
  • ✅ 3. Study without distractions.
  • ✅ 4. Take a 5-minute break.
  • ✅ 5. Test yourself on what you learned.
  • ✅ 6. Repeat if you still have work to do.
